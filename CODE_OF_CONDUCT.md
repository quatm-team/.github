# Code of conduct

Quatm is a scientific software community. We want participation to be useful,
direct and safe for people with different backgrounds, institutions and levels
of experience.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experimental constraints and
  institutional requirements.
- Give technical criticism about code, data or design, not about the person.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim about hardware,
  timing or data is uncertain.
- Respect confidentiality. Do not publish another laboratory's data,
  configuration, device identifiers or unreleased results.
- Keep discussions on topic and move sensitive security reports to the private
  channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing private information without consent, deliberate disruption of
discussions, and encouraging unsafe laboratory or data practices.

## Scope

This code applies in Quatm repositories, issue trackers, discussions, review
comments, chat channels and any space where someone represents Quatm.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to **contact@quatm.com**. Include relevant links or context and
state whether the report is confidential. Maintainers will review reports in
good faith, avoid conflicts of interest, and may warn, remove content, restrict
participation or take other proportionate action.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
