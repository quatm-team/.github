- Commercial licensing and engineering services: **sales@quatm.com**
- Security reports: **security@quatm.com** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the issue templates in this repository when the target repository does not
provide its own. Include the Quatm version, operating system, component,
hardware/driver (if relevant) and a minimal reproduction.

For a laboratory outage or a safety concern, contact the team directly instead
of waiting for a public issue.

## What support is not

This is not a guaranteed 24/7 support desk. Response times depend on the
channel, severity and the applicable support or licence agreement. Community
channels are best-effort; commercial terms are defined in the applicable
agreement.
